LPC RESIDENCY HOUR TRACKER - HOW TO USE
=======================================

GETTING STARTED
1. Save this folder somewhere permanent on your computer (for example, Documents).
2. Double-click "LPC Tracker.html". It opens in your web browser. No internet or installation needed.
3. Tip: bookmark the page, or drag it to your bookmarks bar, so it's one click to open.
   Always open it in the same browser (for example, always Chrome) because that's where your hours are saved.

LOGGING HOURS
1. Choose the type of hours:
   - Clinic client      -> counts toward direct client contact (2,000) and total (3,400)
   - School counseling  -> counts toward direct client contact (2,000) and total (3,400)
   - Supervision        -> counts toward supervision (200) and total (3,400).
                           Mark it as Individual or Group.
   - Other / indirect   -> counts toward the total (3,400) only. Use this for notes, paperwork,
                           trainings, and other residency work that isn't face-to-face with clients.
2. Pick the date and enter the hours (use decimals: 0.25 = 15 min, 0.5 = 30 min, 0.75 = 45 min).
   Logging a whole week at once? Switch to "Date range", pick the From and To dates (or click
   "This week" / "Last week" for Mon-Fri), and enter the TOTAL hours, e.g. 40. It's saved as one
   entry in your log.
3. Add an optional note (supervisor, site, number of clients). Don't put client names in notes.
4. Click "Add to log". Everything saves automatically.

To fix a mistake, click "Edit" or "Delete" next to the entry in the Hour log.

KEEPING YOUR HOURS SAFE (IMPORTANT)
Your hours are saved inside your web browser on this computer. If you clear your browsing data,
switch browsers, or get a new computer, they won't come with you automatically.

- Click "Download backup" every week or two. It saves a small file (LPC-hours-backup-DATE.json).
  Email it to yourself or put it in Google Drive, iCloud, or OneDrive.
- To get your hours back, or to move them to another computer or browser, open the tracker
  there and click "Restore from backup..." and choose the most recent backup file.
  (Restoring replaces whatever is currently in the tracker with the backup.)

The tracker will remind you if you haven't backed up in a while.

OTHER FEATURES
- Export to spreadsheet (CSV): opens in Excel or Google Sheets. Useful for your supervisor
  or for filling out licensing paperwork.
- Print / save as PDF: prints your progress and full log.
- Filters above the log: show one type of hours, a date range, or search your notes.
- Settings (bottom of page): change the hour goals if your requirements change, and set your
  residency start date so the weekly average and projected finish date are accurate.

Always confirm the current requirements with the Virginia Board of Counseling. This tracker is
for your personal records and doesn't replace official forms or your supervisor's sign-off.
